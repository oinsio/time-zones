# Glossary: Time selection

Choosing a time of day, and showing each location's day around the reference moment. Decisions: ADR-0003 (absolute hours), ADR-0007 (the day of the reference zone), ADR-0011 (the meeting range); detail in `docs/architecture/domain-model.md` ("Day periods") and `views.md` ("Cards"). See [README](README.md) for the entry format.

- **Reference day** — the span of absolute time from local midnight of the reference zone on the effective instant's date to the next local midnight. It is 23, 24 or 25 hours long. Every day track and hour cell spans it. *Code:* `ReferenceDay`, `getReferenceDay`. *Not:* a calendar date, and not the location's own local day. *Never:* current day, today (unless it is today).
- **Day track** — the horizontal bar on a card that spans the reference day, coloured by the location's day periods, with a handle at the reference moment. All handles stand at the same position, except a handle that is being dragged. *Code:* `DayTrack`, `presentDayTrack`. Its accessibility role is `slider`, and only in that sense is "slider" correct (`locations.timeSliderLabel` «Время — {city}»). *Never:* time slider, timeline, scrubber.
- **Day period marker** — the `AM` / `PM` text of the 12-hour format. *Code:* `DayPeriodMarkers`, `getDayPeriodMarkers`, `LocationRow.localTimeParts`. *Not:* a day period. *Never:* day half.
- **Handle** — the circle on a day track at the position of the reference moment. Dragging it, or tapping the track, selects a time. During a drag, the handle follows the pointer along the track. When released, it settles at the reference moment. *Not:* the grab area of a card. *Never:* thumb, knob.
- **Time selection** — pinning the reference moment to an instant chosen on a day track: by pointer, tap or keys, rounded to a track step and clamped between the start of the reference day and the track end. *Code:* `SELECT_INSTANT`, `useTimeSelection`. *Never:* scrubbing, seeking.
- **Track step** — the 15-minute granularity of a time selection, counted from the start of the reference day. *Code:* `TRACK_STEP_MINUTES`.
- **Track fraction** — a position on a day track as a share 0–1 of the reference day's length. *Code:* `getTrackFraction`, `getInstantAtTrackFraction`.
- **Track value** — the minutes from the start of the reference day to the handle, exposed as the slider value; it ranges from 0 to the track end. *Code:* `getTrackValueMinutes`.
- **Track end** — the last minute of the reference day, one minute before its end in absolute time (23:59 in the reference zone). It is the right end of a day track, the maximum track value and the target of the End key. *Code:* `getTrackEndMinutes`. *Not:* the end of the reference day itself, which is the next local midnight.
- **Track move** — a keyboard step of the handle: next or previous step, an hour forward or back, the day's start or the track end. *Code:* `TrackMove`, `getMinutesAfterTrackStep` (the next or previous track step position, shared by the day track and the meeting handles).
- **Day period** — the part of a location's day decided by the sun's elevation at its coordinates: night below −6°, day from +6°, between them morning until solar noon and evening after it. *RU:* ночь, утро, день, вечер. *Code:* `DayPeriod.NIGHT | MORNING | DAY | EVENING`, `getDayPeriodAt`, token `--color-day-daylight`. *Not:* the working hours (the user's range). *Never:* work, working hours, business hours, office hours, daytime (for this period).
- **Sun elevation** — the angle of the sun's centre above the horizon at given coordinates and instant, in degrees. *Code:* `getSunElevation` (forwards to the sun support), `src/model/sunPosition.ts` (the equations).
- **Solar day** — the span from one solar midnight to the next at given coordinates, with its solar noon (the sun highest) and solar midnight (the sun lowest). *Code:* `SolarDay`, `getSolarDay` (forwards to the sun support), `src/model/sunCalculation.ts` (the equations).
- **Sun support** — the lazily loaded chunk holding the sun calculation and the zone coordinates, kept out of the initial JavaScript; the app loads it before its first render and shows the recovery screen if it fails to load. *Code:* `SunSupport`, `registerSunSupport`, `getSunSupport`, `loadSunSupport` in `src/model/sunRegistry.ts`; the chunk is `src/model/sunSupport.ts`; `mountApp` waits for it. *Never:* sun bundle, sun module (for this chunk).
- **Day-period boundary** — an instant at which a location's day period changes. *Code:* `DayPeriodBoundary`, `getDayPeriodBoundaries`.
- **Blend window** — the span around a day-period boundary where the two colours blend: 30 minutes, clipped at the midpoint to the neighbouring boundary. *Code:* `BlendWindow`, `collectBlendWindows`, `DAY_PERIOD_BLEND_MINUTES`.
- **Offset edge** — in the hour strip only: the line on the left side of the first hour cell that starts at or after a clock change inside the reference day (`HourCell.hasOffsetEdge`).
- **Colour stop** — one position and day colour of a day track's gradient. *Code:* `DayColourStop`, `presentDayColourStops`, `DayPeriodStop`, `getDayPeriodStops`.
- **Hour label** — a location's local time printed under its day track on wide screens, at the start, every 6 hours and at the end of the reference day (`22 · 04 · 10 · 16 · 22`). *Code:* `HourLabel`, `formatHourLabel`.
- **Hour cell** — one absolute hour of the reference day in a grid row, labelled with the location's local start time (`9`, `9:30`) and coloured by the day colouring. A tap sets a one-hour meeting range. *Code:* `HourCell`, `getHourCells`, `PresentedHourCell`, `presentHourStrip`.
- **Hour strip** — the row of hour cells under a grid row's header, over the reference day, with the strip tail when the meeting range needs it; all strips scroll together on a narrow screen. For the keyboard it is the Grid view's slider of a time selection, with the day track's name, value and keys. *Code:* `HourStrip` (`views/grid/HourStrip.tsx`), `PresentedHourStrip`, `useHourGrid`. *Not:* a day track. *Never:* timeline, hour bar.
- **Hour frame** — the frame (accent in Time of day, foreground colour on accent cells, `getHourFrameColour`) across all grid rows around the hour cell of the effective instant; while live it follows the current hour. It lies behind the row header texts and row notes. *Code:* `getHourFrameIndex`, `HourFrame` (`views/grid/HourFrame.tsx`). *Not:* the meeting frame. *Never:* cursor, selection frame, now line.
- **Meeting range** — the optional span from a start instant inside the reference day to an end instant at most 24 hours later, also in the next day, at any whole minute, chosen in the Grid view; the reference moment is pinned to its start; its meeting handles move its ends on the track step and its move handle moves the whole range. Not stored. *RU:* «Встреча». *Code:* `MeetingRange` (`WorldClockState.meeting`), `SET_MEETING_RANGE`, `SET_MEETING_RANGE_WALL_TIMES`, `CLEAR_MEETING_RANGE`, `MOVE_MEETING_RANGE`, `getMeetingRangeAfterMove`, `MEETING_RANGE_MAX_MINUTES`, `getMeetingSpan`, `getLocationMeetingRange`, `useMeetingRange`, `MEETING_START_EQUALS_END`. While it is set in Grid, the grab area, the location menu and the wall-time input of every row are locked. *Never:* slot, event, appointment, interval.
- **Meeting frame** — the green frame across all grid rows around the meeting range; cells outside it are dimmed; a press inside it moves the range. *Code:* `MeetingFrame` (`views/grid/MeetingFrame.tsx`). *Not:* the hour frame.
- **Meeting handle** — a grip on the left or right side of the meeting frame that moves the start or the end onto the track step; stands on the grid rows visible on screen; a slider for assistive technology. The move handle is the third one: a grip turned by a quarter turn on the top line of the frame (or on the line of the meeting bar when the top is out of view) that moves the whole range. *RU:* «Начало встречи» / «Конец встречи» / «Сдвиг встречи». *Code:* `MeetingHandle` (`MeetingHandle.MOVE`), `MoveHandleSlider` (`views/grid/MoveHandle.tsx`), `useMoveHandlePlacement`, `getMeetingRangeAfterHandleMove`, `MeetingHandleSlider` (`views/grid/MeetingHandle.tsx`), `getMeetingHandleRow`, `useVisibleStripRows`. *Not:* the day track's handle, the grab area. *Never:* thumb, knob.
- **Meeting bar** — the header while a meeting range is set, sticky at the top of the viewport: the duration, Share meeting and Clear meeting. *RU:* «Встреча · 2 ч», «Поделиться встречей», «Сбросить выбор встречи». *Code:* `MeetingBar`, `presentMeetingBar`, `useMeetingBar`, `MEETING_BAR_SELECTOR`.
- **Share meeting** — the meeting bar action that opens the share dialog; it shares nothing by itself. A share whose outcome is shared clears the meeting range as Clear meeting does, and the focus goes to Add location. Passing the meeting range on to other people is text only, no link (Q8). *RU:* «Поделиться встречей». *Code:* `useMeetingShare` (open flag and notice), `useMeetingShareDialog` (`onMeetingShared`), `TextSharing`.
- **Share dialog** — the modal dialog where the user picks the locations, the share language and the share zone label of a meeting, reads the preview and sends it. *RU:* «Поделиться встречей». *Code:* `MeetingShareDialog`, `LazyMeetingShareDialog`, `useMeetingShareDialog` (the session: ticks, share language, rows, text, share), `useMeetingShareSession`, `useMeetingShare` (open flag).
- **Meeting invitation** — the meeting range with the locations it is shared with, in list order; it holds no language and no text. *Code:* `MeetingInvitation`, `getMeetingInvitation`.
- **Share destination** — a place the share dialog can send a meeting invitation to; the dialog shows one button per available destination. Today: the text destination. *RU:* «Поделиться». *Code:* `ShareDestination`, `ShareDestinationId`, `createShareDestinations`; its label `presentShareDestinationLabel`, its icon `SHARE_DESTINATION_ICONS`.
- **Meeting share text** — the text Share meeting sends, in the share language: the weekday, date and duration, then one line per included location with its zone parts in brackets, its local span and, when its date differs, the day shift. *Code:* `presentMeetingShareText`, `presentMeetingShareLine`, `presentMeetingShareZoneParts`.
- **Midnight marker** — a mark with the new date where a location's local midnight falls on its track or row. In the Grid view it is the hour cell whose start is the first instant of a new local date: it shows the month over the day (`МАР` / `8`) instead of `0` and is shaded; the first cell of the reference zone's row shows a plain `0`; its first cell in the strip tail is a midnight cell (`МАР` / `9`). *Code:* `HourCell.midnightDate`, `PresentedHourCell.midnightMarker`.
- **Wall-time input** — typing an exact wall-clock time in a location card. While digits are typed, it inserts the `:` separator after the hour. *RU:* «Ввести время». Locked in Grid while a meeting range is set. *Not:* a time selection, which is a choice on a day track in track steps. *Never:* time picker, time edit. *Code:* `SET_WALL_TIME`, `parseWallTimeInput`, `formatTypedWallTimeText`, `useWallTimeInput`, `WallTimeInput`.
- **Meeting range input** — typing the start and the end of the meeting range in a grid row's local time; the span opens it while a meeting range is set; its fields insert the separator as the wall-time input does. *RU:* «Ввести время встречи»; its fields «Время начала» / «Время окончания». Its messages are «Начало и окончание встречи совпадают» and «Начало встречи должно быть внутри дня»; an end earlier than the start ends the next day. *Code:* `SET_MEETING_RANGE_WALL_TIMES`, `confirmMeetingRangeInput`, `MeetingRangeInput`. *Not:* the wall-time input; not the meeting handles «Начало встречи» / «Конец встречи». *Never:* time picker, range picker.
- **Wall-time target** — the row a wall-time input was typed in: a location. It decides where the wall-time hint is shown. *Code:* `WallTimeTarget`.
- **Working day** — a Monday-to-Friday local date. *Code:* `WORKING_WEEKDAYS`, `isWorkingDay`.
- **Working time** — the instants of a working day between the start and the end of the working hours in a location's local time; a cell is working when it covers at least half. *Code:* `isWorkingTime`, `getWorkingTimeSpans`, `getWorkingTimeStops`, `getHourCellWorkingFlags`, `WORKING_CELL_MIN_SHARE`.
- **Day colour** — the colour of a track position or an hour cell: a day period, no day period, plain, working time or non-working time. *Code:* `DayColour`, `DayColour.NO_DAY_PERIOD` (the accent colour of a location without day periods), `DayColourStop`, `presentDayColourStops`, `presentHourCellColour`, `DAY_COLOUR_VARIABLES`.
- **Strip tail** — the first cells of the next reference day that follow the reference day's cells in every hour strip while a meeting range reaches past midnight, plus one spare cell; none without a meeting range. *RU:* хвост. *Code:* `getStripTailCellCount`, `getNextReferenceDay`, `getHourCells` (the tail argument). *Not:* a second day grid. *Never:* overflow, extension.
- **Meeting move** — moving the whole meeting range: start and end shift by the same amount, the length never changes; by the move handle, by a press on the frame or by keys. *RU:* «Сдвиг встречи». *Code:* `MOVE_MEETING_RANGE`, `useMeetingFrameMove` (`views/grid/useMeetingFrameMove.ts`), `getMeetingRangeAfterMove`. *Not:* a date change; not the move of a row. *Never:* shift, drag the meeting.

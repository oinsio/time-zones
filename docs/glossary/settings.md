# Settings

The user's preferences and the page that changes them. Stored by their own repository (`src/preferences/`). See [README](README.md) for the entry format.

- **Settings page** — the page with the Gnomish Factory credit, the theme preference, the hour format, the view, the day colouring, its day palette group and its working hours row, the zone label, the interface language, the deploy time, the zone rules release line and the city data attribution. *Code:* `SettingsPage`, `AppPage.SETTINGS`. *RU:* «Настройки».
- **Settings button** — the header button that opens the settings page. *Code:* `SettingsButton`. *RU:* «Настройки».
- **Preferences** — the user's choices kept on the device, apart from the location list: the theme preference, the hour format, the view mode, the day colouring, the day palette, the working hours, the zone label, the share language, the share zone label and the interface language. *Code:* `Preferences`, `src/preferences/`, `PreferencesRepository`.
- **Theme preference** — light, dark or system. System follows the system color scheme. Shown as icons only, in the order System, Light, Dark (`THEME_PREFERENCE_ORDER`). *Code:* `ThemePreference`, `THEME_PREFERENCE_ORDER`. *RU:* «Тема»: «Системная», «Светлая», «Тёмная».
- **Hour format** — how times of day are written, 12-hour or 24-hour; shown by the pictograms `12` and `24` (`HOUR_FORMAT_PICTOGRAMS`). *Code:* `HourFormat`, `HOUR_FORMAT_PICTOGRAMS`. *RU:* «Формат времени»: «12 часов», «24 часа».
- **Zone label** — the preference that decides which zone parts the zone line shows, the country, the zone abbreviation and the UTC offset, each on or off, all on by default; it does not affect the search. *RU:* «Обозначение часового пояса»: «Страна», «Аббревиатура», «Смещение UTC»; English interface: "Timezone label", "Country", "Abbreviation", "UTC offset"; with every part off the example reads «Только город» / "City only"; the toggles follow the order of the zone line (`ZONE_LABEL_PART_ORDER`). *Code:* `Preferences.zoneLabelParts`, `ZONE_LABEL_PART_ORDER`, `ZoneLabelPart`, `SET_ZONE_LABEL_PART`, `useZoneLabelParts`, `ZoneLabelGroup`, `settings.zoneLabelLabel`, `settings.zoneLabelCountry`, `settings.zoneLabelExampleCityOnly`.
- **Deploy time** — the instant the running build of the app was made, fixed into the build, shown on the settings page after the language group, followed by the zone rules release line, then the city data attribution. *RU:* «Развёрнуто …». *Code:* `APP_DEPLOY_INSTANT`, `resolveDeployInstant`, `formatDeployTime`, `useDeployTime`, `DeployTime`, `settings.deployTime`. *Not:* the app revision. *Never:* build number, release date.
- **Zone rules release line** — the plain text after the deploy time that names the active release. *RU:* «Правила часовых поясов: …». *Code:* `ZoneRulesReleaseLine`, `settings.zoneRulesRelease`, `presentZoneRulesRelease`.
- **Toggle group** — a compact settings group in one row: a caption and independent toggle buttons, each on or off; it shares its row layout with the option group (`SETTINGS_GROUP_ROW_CLASS_NAME`). *RU:* группа переключателей. *Code:* `SettingsToggleGroup`.
- **Day palette** — the set of colours of the four day periods, picked in the settings group «Цвета»: Vivid (default) or Muted; it colours the day tracks of Cards and the hour cells of Grid in the Time of day colouring only. *RU:* «Цвета»: «Яркие», «Приглушённые»; English "Colours", "Vivid", "Muted". *Code:* `DayPalette`, `DAY_PALETTE_ORDER`, `Preferences.dayPalette`, `SET_DAY_PALETTE`, `useDayPalettePreference`, `DayPaletteGroup`, `settings.dayPaletteLabel`. *Never:* colour scheme, theme.
- **Palette swatch** — the 24 × 24 px square of a day palette option, four vertical stripes in the night, morning, day and evening colours of that palette. *Code:* `PaletteSwatch`. *Not:* an icon.
- **Option group** — a compact settings group in one row: a caption and mutually exclusive options, exactly one checked, joined in one control; each option is shown by an icon, a palette swatch or a pictogram, or by its name when it has neither (the view group shows the view names), and its name is its accessible name and pointer hint. *RU:* группа выбора. *Code:* `SettingsOptionGroup`, `SettingsOption`, `SETTINGS_GROUP_ROW_CLASS_NAME`.
- **Gnomish Factory** — the tool the app is built with; its landing page is linked from the settings page. A brand name: written `Gnomish Factory` in every interface language, never translated. *Code:* `GNOMISH_FACTORY_LANDING_URL`. *RU:* «Gnomish Factory».
- **Gnomish Factory credit** — the line of the settings page above the theme preference, one link "Built with Gnomish Factory" to the factory's landing page, opened in a new tab. *Code:* `GnomishFactoryCredit`, `settings.gnomishFactoryCredit`. *RU:* «Создано с помощью Gnomish Factory».
- **Interface language** — the language of every interface text, date, country name, city name of search results and city label of a city location. It is chosen in the language group of the settings page, or, when none is chosen, it follows the browser language and then falls back to English. *Code:* `Preferences.interfaceLanguage`, `SET_INTERFACE_LANGUAGE`, `resolveInterfaceLanguage`, `useInterfaceLanguage`, `useActiveInterfaceLanguage`, `InterfaceLanguageGroup`, `LanguageGroup` (the shared block of `views/shared/`), `settings.languageLabel`. *RU:* «Язык интерфейса»; the group caption is «Язык».
- **Locale metadata** — the `_meta` block of a locale file: `code` (equal to the file name, a canonical language tag), `name` (English name, the sort key), `nativeName`, `baseLanguage`, `emoji`. It is never a translation. *Code:* `LocaleMetadata`, `parseLocaleMetadata`.
- **Locale registry** — every locale file whose locale metadata is complete and valid, collected automatically and sorted by `name`. It is the only source of the supported languages, the i18next resources and the settings list. City data is kept for `CITY_NAME_LANGUAGES` only. *Code:* `localeRegistry`, `buildLocaleRegistry`, `RegisteredLocale`, `SUPPORTED_LANGUAGES`.
- **Dialect** — a locale whose `baseLanguage` differs from its `code`; easter-egg languages are dialects. A missing text or language-keyed value comes from the base language, then English. Dates, weekdays, months and country names formatted through `Intl` use the same order as their locale list. *Code:* `resolveLanguageFallbacks`, `pickByLanguageFallback`.
- **City data attribution** — the line at the end of the settings page crediting GeoNames and CC BY 4.0 with a link and naming the dump date of the city data. *RU:* «Данные о городах: GeoNames (14 ноября 2026 г.), CC BY 4.0». *Code:* `CityDataAttribution`, `presentCityDataAttribution`, `CITY_DATA_DATE`, `settings.cityDataAttribution`, `GEONAMES_URL`.
- **Day colouring** — how day tracks and hour cells are coloured, in both views: Plain, Time of day (default; follows the sun) or Working hours. Shown as three icons in the option group. *RU:* «Раскраска»: «Обычная», «По времени суток», «По рабочему времени»; English "Colouring", "Plain", "Time of day", "Working hours". *Code:* `DayColouring`, `DAY_COLOURING_ORDER`, `Preferences.dayColouring`, `SET_DAY_COLOURING`, `useDayColouring`, `DayColouringGroup`, `settings.dayColouringLabel`. *Never:* colour scheme.
- **Working hours** — one start and one end for every location, read in each location's local time, 10:00–18:00 by default. *RU:* «Рабочие часы». *Code:* `WorkingHours`, `DEFAULT_WORKING_HOURS`, `checkWorkingHours`, `SET_WORKING_HOURS`, `useWorkingHoursInput`, `WorkingHoursGroup`, `settings.workingHoursLabel`. *Never:* business hours, office hours.
- **Time range group** — the reusable settings row of a caption and two time fields joined by a dash; it types and reports texts, never reads times. *RU:* группа диапазона времени. *Code:* `SettingsTimeRangeGroup`.
- **Share language** — the language of the meeting share text, chosen in the share dialog; it follows the interface language unless it is pinned, and it changes nothing else. *RU:* «Язык приглашения»; the pin «Запомнить язык приглашения». *Code:* `Preferences.shareLanguage`, `SET_SHARE_LANGUAGE`, `resolveShareLanguage`, chosen in `LanguageGroup`.
- **Share zone label** — the zone parts the meeting share text writes in brackets, the zone abbreviation and the UTC offset, each on or off, both on by default; never the country. It is separate from the zone label. *RU:* «Обозначение часового пояса» in the share dialog. *Code:* `Preferences.shareZoneLabelParts`, `SHARE_ZONE_LABEL_PARTS`, `TOGGLE_SHARE_ZONE_LABEL_PART`.
